01 · Verified badge
Patient app · trust primitive

The single component the business rests on. It is a stamp, not a status: ink-coloured, 1.5px border, never a hue of its own — so it reads as a mark on a document rather than another chip in the state set. It always states who verified and when at full size; the compact form is a summary that must remain tappable through to the full form.

  • Suspension replaces the badge in place — it never sits beside a positive stamp, and the provider card's book action is removed, not greyed.
  • It is the one badge variant permitted a hue, because it is a warning rather than a mark, and it must not be mistakable for the ink stamp at a glance.
  • Copy never states a reason. Reasons are between Pulse and the provider; publishing one is a defamation risk and a support load.
  • Always paired with a forward action, so a suspension is never a dead end for the patient.
  • Patients with an upcoming visit from a suspended provider are contacted by ops, not notified in-app — the app must not create alarm it can't resolve.

Open before build
  • All Tamil copy remains placeholder and needs a native speaker — the state labels especially, since plain-language wording is the whole point of component 04.
  • The provider card is drawn for both the pick a person and the assigned later models because that product decision is still open. Whichever you choose, the other variant stays useful.
