The trace is bent into a ring and the beat closes it. One shape carries both ideas the service is built on: continuity of care, and the seal that says a person was verified. The gap at the lower-left is deliberate — the loop is open until the visit completes.

02 · Lockups, clearspace, minimum size

Clearspace equals the ring's stroke gap — half the mark's height on every side. Stroke weight steps up below 32px so the beat survives; never render the mark below 20px or place the wordmark below 14px.
