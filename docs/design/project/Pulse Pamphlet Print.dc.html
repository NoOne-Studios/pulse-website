Only the 108 disclaimer and the QR caption sit below 10 pt, and neither carries information the reader needs.
One variable element
The tinted geography band is the only place a neighbourhood is named. When coverage expands, that band is the sole plate change — headline, stamp and CTA are geography-free so they never expire.
Phone before QR, always
The number is 25 pt and blue; the QR is 24 mm, grey-bordered, and captioned with where it goes. The caption ends by telling the reader they don't need it.

What is deliberately absent
No price, no approval time, no response window, no count of families, no testimonial, no doctors, no consultation, no symptom list, no "call us if you notice…". A printed figure is a promise with no correction path.
Doctors: excluded from this run
Doctor consultation is Phase 1 but appears nowhere here (MKT-06 §5, PRD-04 §6.4). Print advertising of doctors carries tighter regulation than the web, and the piece cannot be recalled. It re-enters only after verified doctors exist and legal sign-off lands — which is a new print run, not an amendment.
Four questions, four blocks
Headline answers what is this. The bordered stamp answers why trust it. "Asking costs nothing" answers what does it cost to find out. The number answers what do I do now. Nothing on the sheet answers a fifth question.

Rules that make it survive
Blue text only at 25 pt or above and never below 60% ink coverage, so it copies as solid dark rather than mush. No reversed white-on-blue type anywhere — reversed Tamil at 11 pt fills in on a copier. No tint below 4% (invisible) or above 12% (turns to grey soup under text). Rules are 1.2 pt where they carry meaning; nothing thinner than 0.75 pt appears at all.
Copier-ready master
Supply the desk a single-colour black PDF alongside the press file. Same layout, blue mapped to 100% K, geography band at 8% K. Staff copying the mono master beats staff copying the colour piece.

Trade-off
For: pockets and handbags without folding, sits properly in a counter rack, and the cover panel does one job. Better for CHN-002 racks.

Against: the discharge desk reads a 99 mm panel as a flyer, not a document; Tamil at 12 pt in an 81 mm measure gives 5–6 words a line and the headline breaks badly; folds cost more and mis-fold; and the four answers get split across panels the reader may never open. A creased trifold in a handbag loses its CTA panel to wear.
Third option, rejected
A4 trifold gives Tamil the most room but is the wrong object at a discharge desk — nothing that size goes in a handbag unfolded, and it reads as a hospital form. Keep A4 for a future ward-noticeboard sheet instead.
Two-version alternative (bilingual strategy B)
Separate all-Tamil and all-English pamphlets. Gains: each face doubles to two sides, so Tamil breathes and the services get their sub-lines back; a reader never holds a face they can't read. Costs: two SKUs, two plate sets, roughly 1.6–1.9× unit cost at small runs; and the real cost is distribution — a discharge clerk holding two stacks has to guess a family's language out loud, in front of them, at the worst moment of their week. That guess is worse than a wasted reverse side. Recommend the single object; revisit only if Tamil pickup ever justifies a Tamil-only long run.
05 · How the other two routes differ
CHN-004 · Discharge desk — primary, as drawn
Handed over, face up, Tamil showing. Flat A5 so it slides into the discharge folder with the paperwork the family is already carrying. No rack furniture needed.
CHN-002 · Clinic, lab and pharmacy racks
A rack hides the lower two-thirds. Move the wordmark and headline into the top 60 mm and repeat the number there in small; the reader must recognise the piece from its head alone. Same trim, same reverse plates — one alternate front. Add a counter stand-card if a partner will take one.
CHN-003 · Community distribution
Lowest intent, longest gap to use, most likely to be read at home a week later — so WhatsApp moves up beside the phone number at equal weight, and the geography band moves above the stamp, because relevance is the first question here, not trust. Consider a cheaper 130 gsm stock at higher volume.

No blocker, but not the family the brief assumed. And if any Tamil face ever fails to clear, the substitution is Noto Sans + Noto Sans Tamil (OFL): a near-identical humanist grotesk with a fuller Tamil range, roughly 2% wider, which this layout absorbs at 12 pt without reflowing a block.
07 · Where print and the design system disagree

Five places where a rule from the screen system was deliberately broken or reinterpreted. Each needs a call before plates.

Blue = action only
On paper nothing is tappable, so the rule can't hold literally. Kept in spirit: blue appears on exactly two things — the mark, and the phone number. It is the reader's cue that the number is the thing to do, and it is used nowhere as decoration.
Radius 12 px on cards
Dropped. There are no cards in print — separation comes from 1.2 pt rules, which is what the system already says it prefers. The stamp box is a square-cornered ruled box; rounded corners at this size read as a sticker.
1.5 px stamp border
Becomes 1.2 pt. Below about 1 pt a rule breaks up on uncoated stock and disappears entirely on a photocopy — and the stamp's whole credibility is carried by its border weight.
Pill-shaped verified chip
Grown into a full-width ruled block with a line of explanation. A 12 pt pill on paper is a decoration; the print reader has no product context, so the claim has to say what it means. The tick keeps its glyph, so it survives greyscale.
Body 15 px baseline
Not a like-for-like conversion. 15 px ≈ 11.25 pt, below the print floor for a stressed or elderly reader. Print body is 12 pt and the layout was cut to fit it — the Tamil service list lost its sub-descriptions to hold the size.
08 · What print freezes — risks to sign off
The CTA cannot be tested
One number, no variants, no reversal. If the line is unstaffed at 7 pm the piece is actively harmful, so the printed hours are a commitment to rostering, not a marketing line. Run the number as a dedicated print line so pickup is attributable without printing anything measurable on the sheet.
The neighbourhood list dates the piece
Naming five areas (WEB-D14) means the run expires when the sixth opens. Contained to one band on each face; print quantities you expect to exhaust in one coverage epoch, and treat leftover stock as scrap rather than overprinting a correction.
Any figure becomes a promise
Which is why there is no price, no approval time, no turnaround, and no count of anything. The only numbers on the sheet are a phone number, a WhatsApp number, opening hours, and 108.
Tamil copy has no undo
Every Tamil string here is placeholder at the right length and must be written and proofread by a native speaker — including the wordmark transliteration, still unsigned on the identity sheet. A typo on screen is a patch; here it is the whole run. Budget two proofreading passes and one read-aloud with a caregiver before plates.
The QR outlives its destination
Point it at a stable short URL you control and can redirect, never at a campaign path. Printed QRs are read months later.
Handed over inside a hospital
A family may read this as hospital-endorsed. Nothing here claims affiliation, carries a partner logo, or resembles a discharge form — deliberately. Any hospital-specific version needs that hospital's written sign-off before it exists.
