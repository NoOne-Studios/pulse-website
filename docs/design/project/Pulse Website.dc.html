The problem is real: every headline, card and tagline on this site promises that care comes to the house, and a video call does not. All three keep the delivery-mode label doing the per-service work, so the headline never has to carry an asterisk.

English · 390px

Talk to a doctor — at the house, or on video

A full consultation with a registered doctor, and a referral onward if one is needed. You choose where it happens; the price for each is told to you before anything is booked.

Two ways it happens

On video
A scheduled video consultation

You pick a time and a doctor calls at it. Useful for a review after a hospital visit, a look at a report you already have, or a follow-up that does not need an examination. No app account, no waiting room.

At the house
A doctor at your parents’ home

The doctor comes to the house and examines in person. Booked the same way as a nursing or physiotherapy visit, and it costs more than a call — you are told which before anything is fixed.

What a consultation includes

The doctor’s own assessment

In person or on the call. Pulse arranges the consultation; it does not take part in it.

A written note afterwards

What was discussed, kept on the same record as every other visit — so the nurse who comes on Thursday is not starting from nothing.

A referral onward, if one is needed

The doctor can refer you to home nursing, home physiotherapy or a home lab collection inside Pulse. You still confirm the time and the price yourself.

A record of a prescription, if the doctor writes one

The doctor issues any prescription through their own means. Pulse keeps a record of it for your reference, and does not issue, generate or send prescriptions.

You ask for a service, never for a symptom

Nothing here asks what is wrong. There is no symptom checker, no questionnaire about the patient and no “find the right doctor for your problem”. You choose a consultation, and where a doctor’s specialisation has been verified you may ask for that specialisation by name — but nothing on this site suggests which one you need. That judgement belongs to a doctor, not to a form.

What this is not

Not an emergency service. Not medical advice — nothing on this page or anywhere else on this site tells you what to do about a symptom. We do not name or picture individual doctors as a way of advertising.

How a doctor is verified

Before a first consultation: NMC or State Medical Council registration, the qualification certificate, and the specialisation to be listed — each confirmed with the body that issued it. Identity and police verification as for every other professional.

நீங்கள் தேர்வு செய்யும் நேரத்தில் மருத்துவர் அழைப்பார். மருத்துவமனை வருகைக்குப் பிறகு, அல்லது கையில் உள்ள அறிக்கையை ஒரு முறை பார்க்க, அல்லது தொடர் ஆலோசனைக்கு பயனுள்ளது.

வீட்டில்

ஆலோசனையில் என்ன அடங்கும்

மருத்துவரின் சொந்த முடிவு

பிறகு எழுத்தில் ஒரு குறிப்பு

தேவையெனில் ஒரு பரிந்துரை

வீட்டு செவிலியர் பராமரிப்பு, பிசியோதெரபி அல்லது ஆய்வக மாதிரி எடுப்புக்கு பல்ஸ்-ல் பரிந்துரை. நேரமும் விலையும் நீங்களே உறுதி செய்கிறீர்கள்.

மருந்துச் சீட்டு எழுததால், அதன் பதிவு

Placeholder origin paragraph, written at roughly the length the real one should be. It says what happened — a family, a discharge, a week of phone calls — in plain sentences and without a single adjective doing work that a fact should do.

Discharge referrals

For discharge coordinators and ward sisters in and around Adyar. When a patient goes home and the family has nobody arranged, refer them to us and we will take the call from there.

What we do not do

We do not take clinical handover, we do not carry out treatment plans, and we do not ask your staff for records. Care instructions stay between your doctor and the family.

Talk to us about referrals

How a referral works

You give the family our number

Or call us yourself with their consent. No patient details are needed to start — only a name to expect and a neighbourhood.

We speak to the family the same day

We tell them plainly whether we can help and what it will cost, before the discharge if there is time.

The ward hears back

One message telling you whether care was arranged. A referral that goes nowhere is worse for you than no referral.
